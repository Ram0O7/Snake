A few small answers.

Is Snake Arcade free to play?

Yes. The entire game is free, with no account, installation, or download required.

Can I play Snake on my phone?

Yes. Swipe on the game board or tap the direction buttons. On desktop, use your arrow keys or WASD. Press Space or the Pause button to pause.

How do scores and difficulty work?

Every apple earns 10 points. Chill, Normal, and Fast change how quickly the snake moves. Your best score for each pace is stored locally in your browser, not on a global leaderboard. Clearing browser data resets it.
